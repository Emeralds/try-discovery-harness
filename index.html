How it works

1

Import your handoff YAML

Ran /office-hours then /discovery-handoff? Import the YAML and every field — reachable proxy, observable signals, trigger events — prefills. No gstack? Type the hypothesis in by hand; it works the same either way. Get the skill: discovery-harness-skill.

2

Get real people, with the reason why

Research finds companies and people who might actually feel the pain. If it can't state a specific reason why one is worth contacting, it won't suggest reaching out.

3

Send outreach you'd actually send

Drafts are short and honest, grounded only in real evidence. Nothing goes out automatically — there's a daily cap, and you approve every message first.

4

Log the call, update the thesis

Follow-up nudges surface on their own. Passing on someone asks why, so you can see whether the thesis is holding up or needs to change.

FAQ

Why isn't this open source?

It's early. I want to see how it holds up outside my own use before opening the source — might revisit that later.

Why is the installer unsigned?

Solo project, no code-signing budget yet. It's safe, just unfamiliar:

Windows: SmartScreen will say "Windows protected your PC" → click More info → Run anyway.

Mac: Gatekeeper blocks the first launch → right-click (or Control-click) the app → Open → confirm Open in the dialog. Opens normally after that.

Why do I need my own OpenAI/Anthropic API key?

Discovery Harness is local-first and BYOK (bring your own key). Research and drafting calls go straight from your machine to your own OpenAI/Anthropic account, so there's no server in between reading your hypothesis, prospects, or drafts.

Do I need Claude Code / gstack to use this?

No. It's richer with a discovery-handoff YAML import, but every field works filled in by hand too.

Discovery Harness is a personal customer-discovery tool, not a CRM.
