Turn a startup thesis into real companies, real people, and respectful outreach — without running a lead-gen campaign.

Discovery Harness is a local, BYOK desktop app for solo founders doing customer discovery. It's not a CRM. It's not cold-email automation. It's the messy first mile of talking to real people, made a little less overwhelming.

Built for gstack users — works for everyone else too

If you're already running Claude Code's /gstack skill, this is the last step of the chain you're already using.

Run /office-hours on a raw idea, then /discovery-handoff to produce a structured targeting YAML. Import that YAML into Discovery Harness and every field — reachable proxy, observable signals, trigger events — is prefilled. Get the skill: discovery-harness-skill.

Don't have gstack? Fill in a product hypothesis by hand instead — name, thesis, target customer, problem. The app works fully either way; gstack just gives the research prompts richer signal to work against.

The bottleneck usually isn't building

It's everything between an idea and a real conversation:

  • Choosing the right customer segment to investigate
  • Finding relevant people to actually talk to
  • Feeling confident outreach is thoughtful, not annoying
  • Turning vague curiosity into specific discovery questions
  • Keeping a lightweight pipeline of conversations moving
  • Capturing what you learn and updating the thesis from it

How it works

1

Start with a thesis

Import a discovery-handoff YAML, or type in a hypothesis by hand — who has the pain, why now, what you need to learn.

2

Find companies and people

Research finds real companies and real people who might feel the pain — always with a stated "why this person?" If it can't explain the reason, it won't suggest reaching out.

3

Draft outreach, you approve it

Short, honest outreach drafts grounded only in real evidence — nothing sends automatically. Daily send cap, human approval required, always.

4

Track replies, log what you learn

Follow-up nudges surface on their own; passing on someone asks why, so patterns in what doesn't fit become visible over time.

FAQ

Why isn't this open source?

It's early and still finding its footing — keeping the source closed for now while it holds up to more than just my own use. Happy to revisit this later.

Why is the installer unsigned?

Solo project, no code-signing budget yet. It's safe, just unfamiliar:

Windows: SmartScreen will say "Windows protected your PC" → click More info → Run anyway.

Mac: Gatekeeper blocks the first launch → right-click (or Control-click) the app → Open → confirm Open in the dialog. Opens normally after that.

Why do I need my own OpenAI/Anthropic API key?

Discovery Harness is local-first and BYOK (bring your own key) — research and drafting calls go straight from your machine to your own OpenAI/Anthropic account. There's no server in between reading your hypothesis, your prospects, or your drafts.

Do I need Claude Code / gstack to use this?

No. It's richer with a discovery-handoff YAML import, but every field works filled in by hand too.
